Using Voice to Save Mothers’ Lives in Uganda

Bringing maternal health information to rural Uganda one voice at a time.

Every day, women in Uganda face preventable maternal complications often because critical health information doesn't reach them in time, or in a language they understand.

At Ad Voice Co., we're using voice technology to bridge that gap. Through IVR, WhatsApp voice notes, and radio, we deliver trusted, localized maternal health messages directly to the people who need them most.

The Problem

Why This Matters:

Uganda’s maternal mortality rate is 189 per 100,000 live births. In rural regions like Kitgum and Moroto, the risk is even higher. Many women lack access to skilled care, can't afford transport, or simply don’t get the right information early enough.

Low literacy rates make written materials hard to understand, and health messaging often fails to reach deep rural communities in languages they relate to.

Our Solution

We’re building a voice-first maternal health platform that speaks to real people in real ways:

  • ✅ IVR system – Call a free line and press a number to hear tips, danger signs, or reminders.
  • ✅ WhatsApp voice notes – Weekly maternal health audio in English and local languages.
  • ✅ Emergency response option – "Press 9" to connect to a WhatsApp call for urgent help.
  • ✅ Track for unwanted pregnancy – Supportive, non-judgmental guidance for young mothers.
  • ✅ Male involvement – Voice content just for fathers and male caregivers.

Why Voice?

  • 📻 87% of households own a radio
  • 📱 Over 70% own a mobile phone
  • 📚 Literacy is still a challenge — but voice cuts through
  • 🗣️ Voice is familiar, trusted, and culturally appropriate

Pilot Plan

Location: Kitgum (Acholi) and Moroto (Karamoja), two of Uganda’s highest-burden maternal health districts.

Timeline:

  • 📅 Phase 1 (Month 1–2): Develop and record content in local languages
  • 📅 Phase 2 (Month 2–3): Set up IVR + WhatsApp channels
  • 📅 Phase 3 (Month 3–4): Community enrollment via clinics & radio
  • 📅 Phase 4 (Month 4–6): Monitor use, gather feedback, improve system

Target Impact:

  • 📞 Reach 500+ women in 6 months
  • 💬 Increase ANC visits & early care-seeking
  • 📊 Collect measurable feedback to inform scale-up
