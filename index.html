Empowering Rural Mothers with Voice Technology

Sauti Mama is a maternal health information platform that uses voice technology to deliver trusted, timely, and culturally relevant information to underserved mothers in rural Uganda.

About Us

We are a passionate team of young innovators brought together by a shared vision: to bridge maternal health information gaps in hard-to-reach Ugandan communities using simple voice technology. Our initiative is powered by AdVoice Uganda, where we combine tech, empathy, and creativity to empower women.

Our team includes Ilobun Faithful Iziengbe, Olu John, Olayinka Taiwo and Niyonsaba Elie—each bringing experience from health, tech, and social impact fields.

Our Solution

Through IVR calls and WhatsApp voice notes, we share maternal health education in local languages tailored for regions with low literacy and smartphone access. Our hybrid model ensures rural mothers receive antenatal and postpartum support on channels they already use.

Pilot Plan

We are launching pilots in Moroto and Kitgum, two districts with significant maternal health disparities. Our aim is to test the delivery of localized content through voice platforms, measure impact, and scale based on feedback from mothers, health workers, and community leaders.
