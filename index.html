Sauti Mama

Bringing maternal health closer to rural Ugandan communities through voice technology.

Problem Solution Pilot Plan About Contact

Problem

Many rural Ugandan women face delays and barriers in accessing timely maternal health information due to low literacy levels, poor health infrastructure, and limited access to skilled care. This contributes to preventable maternal deaths and complications during pregnancy, childbirth, and postpartum periods.

Solution

Sauti Mama is an interactive voice response (IVR) and WhatsApp-based voice messaging system that delivers life-saving maternal health information in local languages. It allows pregnant women and new mothers to access health tips, appointment reminders, and danger sign alerts using basic phones — no internet or reading skills required.

Pilot Plan

We plan to pilot the Sauti Mama system in Moroto and Kitgum districts. These regions were selected due to their high maternal mortality rates and limited digital health access. We will work with local health centers and community health workers to onboard users, monitor adoption, and collect feedback for scaling.
