About Us

Sauti Mama is a maternal health information platform that uses voice technology (IVR and WhatsApp voice notes) to reach underserved mothers in rural Uganda.

With low smartphone and literacy rates in regions like Moroto and Kitgum, we are bridging the information gap by delivering trusted, localized, and timely health content in native languages.

AdVoice Uganda was born out of a shared vision among young innovators committed to solving health communication challenges through technology. Each team member brings a unique skillset, from strategy and content development to community engagement and marketing.

Our Solution

Through partnerships with health professionals and local leaders, Sauti Mama delivers culturally appropriate content in audio format. Whether it's antenatal care tips or postpartum support, we ensure every mother hears what matters most.

We use both inbound and outbound IVR calls and integrate with WhatsApp to create a hybrid channel that fits the communication habits of rural women.
